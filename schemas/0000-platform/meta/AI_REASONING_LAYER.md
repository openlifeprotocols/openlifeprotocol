# AI Reasoning Layer

## Objective

Enable graph-native reasoning on top of ontology entities and relationships.

## Canonical Reasoning Chain

- Goal blocked by Risk
- Risk mitigated by Project
- Project delayed by Commitment
- Commitment conflicts with Time Block

## Decision Intelligence Chain

- Decision
- Evidence
- Alternatives
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Outcome

## Minimum Query Capabilities

- dependency tracing (depends-on, blocks, enables)
- causality tracing (causes, mitigates, fulfills)
- confidence-aware ranking using provenance tiers and confidence
- temporal validity filtering (valid/effective/observed/recorded)

## Required Inputs

- relationship-type ontology entries
- lifecycle definitions for major entities
- temporal context records
- provenance truth tiers and confidence
- decision-intelligence links

## Success Criteria

The model can answer:

- Which active goals are blocked and by what?
- Which mitigations reduced risk fastest?
- Which decision patterns consistently produce positive outcomes?
- Which opportunities are actionable in current time constraints?
