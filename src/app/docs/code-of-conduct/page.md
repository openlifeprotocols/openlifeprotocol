## Our Commitment

The Open Life Protocol community is committed to fostering a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all participants, regardless of:
- Age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression
- Level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance
- Race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior Includes

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Actively listening to others
- Assuming good faith in interactions
- Helping others succeed

### Unacceptable Behavior Includes

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Insulting or derogatory comments or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Unwelcome sexual attention or conduct
- Violence, threats of violence, or violent language
- Intimidation or bullying
- Spam or self-promotion
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

---

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for:
- Clarifying expectations for acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate action in response to violations
- Removing comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this code
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors for violations

---

## Reporting

Violations should be reported to:
**conduct@openlifeprotocol.org**

All reports will be:
- **Treated confidentially** - No identifying information shared
- **Investigated promptly** - Within 48 hours
- **Handled professionally** - With due process

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All spaces managed by the Open Life Protocol project
- Code repositories and documentation
- Community discussions and forums
- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Social media channels
- Conferences and community events
- Private communications when discussing project business

---

## Consequences

Community leaders will determine consequences for violations:
- **Warning** - Verbal or written warning
- **Temporary Suspension** - Removed from community for period
- **Permanent Ban** - Removed from all community spaces

---

## Appeals

Contributors may appeal enforcement decisions by contacting:
**appeals@openlifeprotocol.org**
